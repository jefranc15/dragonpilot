## Actual curve-brake release mechanism: five no-lead events

All five relevant no-lead curve releases in Nov 6 are real V5.0
`_start_staged_release` -> `_clear_hydraulic` calls with
`self.curve_brake_active=True` before reset, and
`release_counter=10` at clear. Four recorded release windows are
completely reproduced at the source-0 BRAKE_STATE level; the severe
`--87` release is one 20 Hz sample early on the vehicle relative to
the replay's clear. The other two `hydraulic_clear` calls are
driver-brake override and noncurve lead-braking release.

V5.0 `longitudinal.py` curve low-demand eligibility is:

```python
low_demand = (
  (not plan.curve_active)
  or plan.brake < P.CURVE_BRAKE_RELEASE
  or curve_speed_error <= P.CURVE_SPEED_ERROR_RELEASE
)
```

Exact V5.0 parameters from `values.py`:
`CURVE_BRAKE_RELEASE = 0.06 m/s^2`,
`CURVE_SPEED_ERROR_RELEASE = 0.20 m/s`,
`RELEASE_CONFIRM_COUNT = 10`, and `BRAKE_MIN = 0.05`.

| Segment | Recorded brake READY relative s | First release-counter source | Source at clear | PID m/s² at clear |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| --86 (1) | 37.994 | cruise | cruise | +0.025 |
| --86 (2) | 46.501 | cruise | cruise | -0.562 |
| --86 (3) | 59.435 | cruise | cruise | -0.270 |
| --87 (1) | 34.854 | cruise | turn | -0.326 |
| **--87 (2)** | **44.274** | **turn** | **turn** | **-0.212** |

In the severe `--87` event, the release counter first increments at
~43.615 s **while `plan.source=turn`** and the planner requests
-0.342 m/s², much stronger than the 0.06 release threshold. Therefore,
the **curve-speed-error criterion**, not the planner-source switch,
initiates this release; the speed is already close to/under its curve
target. At ~43.764 s the planner switches to `cruise` and release
counting persists. The counter reaches 10 by ~44.077 s; source is back
to `turn` by ~44.275 s. Recorded 0x271 BRAKING -> READY is ~44.274 s,
and replay controller clear is ~44.325 s. At clear, the car has
`vEgo≈75.85 km/h`, while `visionTurnSpeed≈78.58 km/h`,
`curve_speed_error=0`, so the speed-target release condition remains
true even though planner and downstream accelerations are negative.

**This explains the release decision. It does not explain the subsequent
engine rev or unexpected positive acceleration.** Do not assume the
source switch or release is inherently a bug: braking generally should
release when target speed is reached.

Earlier raw logs showed the hybrid engine RPM rises during braking,
peaks at ~3,047 after release and the measured acceleration reaches
~+1.29 m/s² with no driver gas input and no positive OP 0x273 mode.
The absence of `IS_ACCEL` does **not** prove zero requested/actual
ECU propulsion torque. Factory-camera bus-2 0x273 does show IS_ACCEL,
but V5.0 repository safety code blocks forwarding this ID to bus 0.
November 6 `pandaStates`: all 359 report Black Panda, Toyota
safety mode, parameter 1, controls allowed, harness flipped; this is
not an independently verified flashed firmware hash.

## Critical November 5 event timing exception

Although aggregate Nov 5 agreement is 99.69%, comparison of the five
recorded BRAKING->READY->BRAKING episodes against source-0 frames shows:

| Recorded route segment | READY gap | Local brake-state matches |
|---|---:|---:|
| --11 | 0.198 s | 4/7 (57.1%) |
| --3 | 0.606 s | 14/16 (87.5%) |
| --12 (afternoon) | 0.599 s | 16/16 (100%) |
| --26 first | 0.600 s | 12/16 (75%) |
| --26 second | 0.601 s | 17/17 (100%) |

In particular, **the replay never reproduces the 0.198-second READY
gap in --11** even though its global gate passes. Do not derive a
brake actuation change from the inferred internal state during that
specific 0.198-second event without better reconstruction and a
per-event fidelity criterion.

## Next safety-focused research steps

1. Keep active V5.0 and Panda unmodified. Do not enable a stronger or
   longer curve brake merely because `turn` -> `cruise` flickers:
   in severe --87, the curve-speed target had already been reached.
2. Make historical replay validation event-local and timing-sensitive.
   Fix the short Nov 5 --11 mismatch before treating those internal
   handoff counters as exact ground truth.
3. Independently verify actual flashed Panda firmware and forwarding
   policy and obtain **validated hybrid ECU motor/engine requested
   torque**, if available, before attributing the positive acceleration
   to camera ACC vs drivetrain vs regen/brake transition.
4. For future passive tests, log curve target speed, release reasons,
   planner source, RPM, pedal, and contemporaneous 0x271/0x273. Never
   intentionally recreate the surge in public-road traffic.
